One {{ site.product.trade }} for both kinds of work

  • Legally aided work

    Bills priced under the Legal Aid Agency's published rates, fees and rules, with each line tied to the rule that prices it. Where the rules leave your firm a choice, your firm's recorded position is applied the same way on every case.

  • Privately funded work

    Bills priced at your firm's own rates and terms: your hourly rates by grade, the way your firm charges for letters, emails and calls, and whatever was agreed with that client.

A case that starts privately funded and moves to legal aid is handled as one case, with each period billed under its own terms.

  • {{ site.product.speed }}
