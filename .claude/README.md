# Knowledge for the customer sign-up portal

Everything about the sign-up portal lives here: what it is for, what is built, what is
next, the decisions taken and the research they rest on. This folder is the home for
it. The sibling repository `automation-portal` holds the wider product (the bill
drafting proof of concept, the legal aid and SRA research, the platform design); it is
referred to from here where needed and is not the place for sign-up portal notes.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| [roadmap.md](roadmap.md) | What is built, what is next, in order, and what waits on Dan |
| [decisions.md](decisions.md) | The decisions taken for the sign-up portal, dated, with who took them |
| [context/account-factory-hand-off.md](context/account-factory-hand-off.md) | The agreed design for "Build portal": how a customer's record reaches the Account Factory. Planned, not built. |
| [context/signup-practice-types.md](context/signup-practice-types.md) | Research: what distinguishes each kind of practice that signs up |
| [context/clsb-costs-lawyer-regulation.md](context/clsb-costs-lawyer-regulation.md) | Research: how the Costs Lawyer Standards Board regulates Costs Lawyers |

Still in `../automation-portal/.claude/` and relied on here: the SRA standards and the
compliance check (`standards/sra-compliance.md`, `context/sra/`), the Account Factory
facts (`context/aws-account-vending-options.md`), and the competing-products research.

How the code works, how to run it and what is tested are in the repository's
`README.md` and `runbooks/`, not here.
